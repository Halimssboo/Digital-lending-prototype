About this platform

Built around affordability, transparency and responsible lending safeguards for African markets.

How we assess

The assessment uses the information you enter to estimate affordability, repayment capacity and relevant financial indicators, using transparent rule-based logic.

What we never collect

No real ID numbers, bank credentials, account passwords or sensitive personal documents are collected or stored.

Limitations

Rates, product ranges and assessment results are illustrative and are not live lending offers, approvals or regulatory assessments.
